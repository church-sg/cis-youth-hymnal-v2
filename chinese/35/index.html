Chinese 35 - 一粒麦子

arrow_forward_ios

一粒麦子 它若不落在地里死了
不论过了多少时候 它仍旧是它自己
它若愿意 让自己被掩埋被用尽
就必结出许多子粒 经历生命的奇迹

~ English ~
Unless a seed consents to fall into the field,
Though all time can pass in waiting, the seed remains alone;
If it consents to be used up, its life to yield,
For the new life it’s creating, soon a harvest is grown!

(Repeat)

To be a seed that falls into the field,
Giving up my life to live a-new!

To obey Your word and follow You!
